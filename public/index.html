A rating queue people actually finish

Scores that know who voted

A 9 from one person is weaker evidence than a 7.5 from everyone. Ratings are calibrated against each person's own baseline, then pulled toward the middle when the evidence is thin.

What to play next

    Library

    Find new games

    The most-played online co-op games on Steam. Add the ones this group might actually play — only games in your library appear in anyone's rating queue, which is what stops a big catalogue from burying the votes that matter.

    Who rates how

    A 7 from one person is not a 7 from another. Scores are taken at face value, so this is context for reading them rather than a correction applied to them.

    Genre track record

    Average score of games you actually played, by genre.

    Who agrees with whom

    Average gap between two people's scores, over the games they have both rated. Pairs need at least five games in common to appear.

    Who is out on a limb

    How far each person sits from what everyone else said, and the genre they are most out of step on.

    Best games you've played

    Where the hours went

    Nobody has an opinion

    Games at least two of you looked at and skipped, and none of you rated. The clearest candidates for removing from the library.

    Biggest arguments

    Games you played where the gap between the highest and lowest score was 2 or more.

    Group

    Rating coverage

    How much of the library each person has actually scored. Everything on the Play next tab is only as trustworthy as the thinnest bar here.

    Members

    Placeholder rows are imported identities nobody has claimed yet.

    Settings

    Invite

    Steam playtime

    Group hours are estimated from Steam: the smallest playtime among the people who actually played a game. That is an upper bound on time you played it together — Steam records how long each of you played, never when or with whom. A figure typed by hand in the Library always wins over the estimate.
