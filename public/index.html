      Steam playtime

      Group hours are estimated from Steam: the smallest playtime among the people who actually played a game. That is an upper bound on time you played it together — Steam records how long each of you played, never when or with whom. A figure saved in the Library stays put; accepted estimates remain visibly approximate.

      Not on Steam? Add it by hand

      No trailer or store art, and it stays out of the fit check unless you set a player count. You can link it to Steam later from the Library if it turns up.

      Import from Steam

      Review your owned games and wishlist before anything is added. Played games start as Sampled; unplayed and wishlisted games start in Backlog. Steam supplies hours and last-played dates when available. Achievement analysis can add an approximate first-played date and flag games with verified ending evidence as Likely finished.
