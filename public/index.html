A rating queue people actually finish

One game at a time with the Steam trailer embedded, driven entirely from the keyboard. Forty unrated games takes about three minutes.

Scores that know who voted

A 9 from one person is weaker evidence than a 7.5 from everyone. Ratings are calibrated against each person's own baseline, then pulled toward the middle when the evidence is thin.

Player-count reality check

Set how many of you there are and anything that can't fit the group gets flagged before you buy it, with couch-only titles marked separately rather than binned.

Per-person taste profiles, genre track records, and a chart of the games you disagreed about most violently.
